# Syntyr Premium Asset License — DRAFT, not an operative contract

**Draft for counsel review.** Nothing here changes the MIT license on the open-source Syntyr CSS library, its themes, core components, fixes, or examples. This license would apply only to individually identified premium template/layout/asset files that are authored separately and sold as a commercial product.

## 1. Eligible assets and proof of purchase

The product page and delivered manifest must identify which files are premium and list any third-party notices. The MIT-covered `syntyr.*.css` files retain their MIT license even when included in a paid bundle. A valid purchase grants the buyer the rights below to premium files as delivered.

## 2. End-product rights (suggested)

The named licensed developer(s) may use, edit, and incorporate premium files into an unlimited number of final public websites, web applications, internal business tools and client projects. They may distribute the finished *end product* to a client, including assets reasonably needed to operate the website. They may keep using the versions bought indefinitely. One-time product purchase includes updates for 12 months only unless a product page states otherwise. Hosting and publication of built end products do not require additional fees.

## 3. Team scope

Single-developer license: one named developer. Team license: up to five named developers working for the purchasing organization; contractors count as developers if they directly edit premium source assets. Organization and team licensing scope should be explicit at purchase; upgrades can be offered.

## 4. Restrictions on separately licensed premium assets only

The buyer may not resell, publish, share or redistribute premium source assets as a standalone UI kit, template pack, design system, asset repository, website/theme builder, generator, or other product whose principal value is providing the premium source files to third parties. They may not grant others independent rights to extract the premium source files to build unrelated end products, except via their own valid licenses. For app stores, white-label SaaS, redistribution-as-template, or embedded page builders, offer individually negotiated rights.

**These restrictions do not apply to any MIT-licensed files or code.** Users remain free to sell, fork, repackage, and redistribute the MIT core in any way its license permits, including in competitor products.

## 5. Third-party IP and branding

Do not include trademarked entertainment logos, movie stills, branded names, proprietary fonts, or third-party icons without documented permission. The brand name is provisional; run a trademark clearance search before launch. Unless otherwise agreed, the license grants no rights to producer trademarks or brand identity.

## 6. Warranty, support, updates, refunds

Specify the applicable warranty disclaimer, promised support channel, update period, price/taxes, and refund terms on the checkout page. Make a separate written agreement for customization, bespoke support, redistributable SDKs or maintenance SLAs. Do not invent a lifetime support commitment.

## 7. Hierarchy

The MIT license controls all files explicitly distributed under MIT. This commercial license controls only the separately designated premium assets. No provision attempts to restrict permissions already granted under MIT. On conflict, the scope of the files and copyright ownership determine which license applies.
