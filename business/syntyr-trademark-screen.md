# SYNTYR: preliminary brand-risk record (not legal clearance)

**Status: unresolved as of 2026-11-14.** This document records preliminary
findings, not an opinion that SYNTYR is registrable or free to use commercially.

- Exact-name indexed web searches did not reveal a prominent competing CSS
  framework. That is not evidence of available trademark rights.
- SYNTER is an existing name used by a software development service
  (https://synter.ai/) and by technology businesses in Brazil.
- SYNCYR has been reported as a UK software/SDK trademark application.
  Current status, classes and goods/services require authoritative checking.
- Domain availability is not proof of trademark ownership.
- No complete live USPTO, INPI Brazil or WIPO search has been verified.

## Clearance gate before paid branding

1. Search exact and phonetic variants SYNTYR, SYNTER, SYNCYR and similar marks
   in https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/, https://servicos.busca.inpi.gov.br/marcas/,
   and https://branddb.wipo.int/.
2. Check Class 9 (downloadable software) and Class 42 (software services),
   plus any related goods/services, for active registrations, applications and
   meaningful common-law use.
3. Document results with filing numbers, status dates, jurisdictions and
   owners; obtain legal review appropriate for intended markets.
4. Do not claim registration or clear the commercial launch gate without evidence.
