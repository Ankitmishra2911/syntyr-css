# Cyberline — preliminary name-risk screen (not legal clearance)

**As of 2026-11-14: pending clearance, paid branding on hold.**

This is a documented preliminary search, not an opinion about infringement,
registrability, or ownership. Existing MIT code can continue to be developed,
but do **not** sell a product as Cyberline or invest in a brand campaign until
the name is cleared for the specific goods/services and markets.

## Findings and evidentiary limits

1. **U.S., exact word CYBERLINE:** a historical application exists, serial
   **90072295**, filed July 24, 2020, for downloadable social/business
   networking software and other goods/services. The third-party Justia
   record shows an event/status from **August 16, 2021**, not a reliable
   live-status finding for 2026.
   - Secondary listing: https://trademarks.justia.com/900/72/cyberline-90072295.html
   - Authoritative **live** search: https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/
   - Official case-status system, search serial 90072295:
     https://tsdr.uspto.gov/
2. **Other public use:** the word appears in the name of unrelated technology
   users/businesses. Existence alone is not proof of an actionable conflict.
   Search results or ownership pages are not exhaustive rights searches.
3. **Brazil:** the INPI's searchable trademark database is accessible at
   https://servicos.busca.inpi.gov.br/marcas/ but **no complete live INPI search
   result was verified** in this investigation. The public search interface
   listed its data currency as July 7, 2026 when checked in November; absence
   from a general web query therefore cannot clear the mark. The official
   INPI guide explains how to search similar marks:
   https://www.gov.br/inpi/pt-br/servicos/marcas/guia-basico

## Why this is more than a handle check

The USPTO says similarity can involve visual appearance, pronunciation,
meaning and commercial impression, and related goods/services need not be
in the same international class. A dead federal application is not the same
as proof that a name is legally available: common-law commercial use can
still matter. See the official guidance:
https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching

## Go/no-go checklist

- [ ] Search exact, plural, phonetic and related marks in the live USPTO
      trademark interface. Record the query, timestamp, results, live/dead
      statuses and goods/services; review serial 90072295 in TSDR.
- [ ] Search the live INPI database for exact/radical/phonetic equivalents
      across relevant software, software-design and template services.
- [ ] Search common-law usage in the U.S./Brazil, GitHub, domains, npm and
      similar registries, including visually and phonetically similar names.
- [ ] Document likely product categories and target markets for review.
- [ ] Obtain qualified legal review where appropriate, then decide whether
      to retain the provisional Cyberline name or choose a new one.
- [ ] Before any actual paid launch, publish customer terms, support scope,
      delivery commitments and refunds separately from the MIT core.
